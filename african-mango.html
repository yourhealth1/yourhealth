What is African Mango?

African Mango (Irvingia gabonensis) is a fruit native to West and Central Africa. Its seeds — called "dika nuts" — have been used traditionally for generations. Modern research has studied the seed extract for its effects on weight management, appetite, and cholesterol levels.

The supplement works by supporting leptin sensitivity (the hormone that tells your brain you're full) and slowing digestion — helping you feel satisfied longer and naturally eat less without feeling deprived.
