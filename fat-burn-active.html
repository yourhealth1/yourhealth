What is Fat Burn Active?

Fat Burn Active is a natural thermogenic supplement — meaning it's designed to gently raise your body's calorie-burning rate (thermogenesis) while also supporting metabolism and energy levels. It's not a "magic pill" — it works best as part of a healthy lifestyle.

The formula combines natural ingredients known to support fat metabolism, including extracts that help your body use stored fat for energy more efficiently, especially during exercise.
